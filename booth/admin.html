Who the visitor meets

The face on screen and the voice it speaks with. Each language has its own voice — pick one that matches the avatar, or visitors notice the mismatch before they notice anything else.

How it talks

Visitors are standing up in a loud hall. Shorter answers get listened to; long ones get walked away from.

Greets once per visitor, not once per answer. Turn this off if the stand is busy and you want answers only.

Visitors walk away without pressing "new visitor". After this long the conversation clears itself, so the next person doesn't inherit it.

What it knows

The assistant answers only from the Devoteam knowledge base. Anything you add here is added to that — so answers using it still show a source.

Write plain sentences, one fact per line. Good for things too new or too local for the knowledge base — stand number, demo times, who to ask for.

Tells the assistant how to behave, not what is true. Leave empty unless you have a reason.

If something breaks

Networks fail at exhibitions. This is what a visitor gets instead of a frozen screen — set it once and forget it.

Keep it short and point at a human. A visitor who is told where to go has been helped; one who is told "an error occurred" has not.
