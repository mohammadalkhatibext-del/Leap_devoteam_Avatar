## 2. Company overview and identity

Devoteam is a premium technology consulting firm that helps organisations across EMEA design, build, and run cloud infrastructure, data platforms, cybersecurity frameworks, agentic AI systems, and digital workplaces.

**Standard corporate boilerplate (current, as used in 2025 to 2026 press releases):**

> Devoteam is a premium consultancy firm driving digital business and transformation through innovative technology. Tech native for over 30 years, we deliver lasting results in Cloud, Data, Cyber and AI for industries and public institutions across EMEA. Strong partnerships have always been central to our DNA. At Devoteam, tech entrepreneurship is at the core of our values, fostering our spirit as a learning company. Within this culture, we attract and train top professionals, creating high talent density across our 11,000 specialists.

**Earlier boilerplate (2024 to 2025 era, still widely quoted):**

> Devoteam is an AI-driven tech consulting firm specialising in cloud platforms, cyber, data, and sustainability. Tech native for almost 30 years, Devoteam guides businesses through sustainable digital transformation to deliver value. With over 11,000 tech architects in more than 25 countries across Europe, the Middle East, and Africa, Devoteam is committed to using technology to serve people.

**What makes Devoteam distinctive (as the company positions itself):**

1. **Tech specialists, not generalist consultants.** All Devoteam employees are technology specialists. 100% of Devoteamers are certified in generative AI. Around 70% hold industry certifications from the key partners (AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft, ServiceNow). In 2024 alone consultants earned more than 7,000 new certifications. The engineering team collectively holds over 23,000 industry certifications.
2. **Top-tier partner status across multiple ecosystems.** Diamond with Google Cloud, Premier Tier with AWS, top-tier with Microsoft, Elite with ServiceNow, Elite with Databricks. Devoteam describes itself as multi-cloud by design rather than tied to a single vendor.
3. **Scale with agility.** Large enough to run major multi-country programmes, structured to still deliver tailored solutions.
4. **Learning company culture.** Tech entrepreneurship as a core value, with heavy investment in training and certification (for example, the one-month Devoteam G Cloud University onboarding programme).
5. **"Tech for People."** Devoteam frames technology as a means to empower people rather than an end in itself.

**Founder philosophy, Stanislas de Bentzmann, on partner strategy:**

> We only work with the most innovative players in the market. Our strategy is not to be generalists. We aim to specialise on a few topics by partnering only with the most advanced software vendors.

### 7.2 What a Forward Deployed Engineer is (explainer for visitors)

A Forward Deployed Engineer is an engineer who works embedded inside the customer's environment rather than remotely from a delivery centre. Instead of handing over a design document, the FDE sits with the client's teams, connects the AI platform to the client's real systems and data, debugs live production issues, and iterates on the solution until it works in the customer's actual operating conditions. The model exists because the hardest part of enterprise AI is rarely the model itself; it is the integration, governance, and last-mile fit with messy real-world systems. Devoteam's FDE programme pairs its own specialists with Google Cloud's technical experts to do exactly this work on Gemini Enterprise deployments.

### 7.3 Devoteam's pre-built agents

Built on the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, on a foundation of security and compliance, and using the Agent2Agent (A2A) protocol. The initial launch of outcome-focused assets includes three agents:

1. **Demand Sensing Agent.** Helps leaders improve forecast accuracy and optimise inventory. It analyses sales and real-time market signals to provide immediate demand insights, enabling proactive adjustments.
2. **Network Incident Investigation Agent.** Unifies disparate connections and autonomously remediates network anomalies early to prevent escalation, improves routing, and reduces manual investigation of network faults and outages.
3. **Request for Proposal (RFP) Analysis Agent.** Helps sales teams draft proposals, using centralised knowledge to rapidly generate tailored and compliant proposal drafts. It applies scoring automation, competitive intelligence, and data extraction to prioritise opportunities aligned with business objectives, then produces high-quality, consistent bid responses.

Devoteam has also published agents on the Google Cloud Marketplace, and the Demand Sensing Agent and Request for Proposal Agent are available as partner-built agents in Gemini Enterprise (announced 22 April 2026).

## 18. Glossary

- **Agentic AI:** AI systems that act autonomously to complete multi-step tasks, rather than only responding to prompts.
- **A2A (Agent2Agent) protocol:** an open protocol that lets AI agents communicate and coordinate across platforms and vendors.
- **AWT:** Agentic Workplace Transformation, Devoteam's practice for modernising workplaces with Google Workspace and Gemini Enterprise.
- **ADAPT:** Devoteam's methodology to move from validated business challenge to production-ready AI in four weeks.
- **AI Factory:** Devoteam service establishing AI policies, guidelines, and best practices so client teams can build their own agentic solutions.
- **CNAPP:** Cloud Native Application Protection Platform.
- **Diamond Partner:** the highest tier in Google Cloud's Partner Network programme, introduced in 2026.
- **FDE:** Forward Deployed Engineer, an engineer embedded in the customer's environment.
- **Gemini Enterprise:** Google's platform integrating AI models such as Gemini to let users build, deploy, and use custom agents across connected enterprise data. Formerly known as Google Agentspace.
- **GreenOps:** practices for reducing the environmental footprint of cloud operations.
- **MSP:** Managed Services Provider.
- **RAG:** Retrieval-Augmented Generation, grounding AI answers in a defined set of trusted documents.
- **VOI:** Value of Investment.
- **Vision 2030:** Saudi Arabia's national transformation programme.
